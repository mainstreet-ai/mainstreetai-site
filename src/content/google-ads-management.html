What usually goes wrong

On a real account I audited, 48 percent of the entire budget was going to the Display Network: banner ads shown to people who were not searching for anything. It produced zero conversions. Every keyword was set to broad match, so the searches actually coming in were things like free template and how to do it yourself, from people who were never going to buy.

None of that is exotic. It is what happens when an account is set up with the default settings and then left alone.

What I check first
