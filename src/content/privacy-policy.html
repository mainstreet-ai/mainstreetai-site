Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

MainStreet AI LLC ("MainStreet AI," "we," "us," or "our") provides website development, AI phone answering, and digital marketing services to small businesses. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have, whether you're visiting our website, contacting us directly, or interacting with the AI phone service we operate on behalf of one of our clients.

Information we collect

We collect information in a few different ways:

  • Information you give us directly, such as your name, business name, email address, phone number, and message when you fill out a contact form or reach out to us.
  • Information collected through our AI phone answering service, when you call a business that uses MainStreet AI's phone agent. This can include your phone number, the content of your call (processed to understand and respond to your request), appointment details, and any message you leave. This information is used to serve that business's customers, not for MainStreet AI's own marketing.
  • Basic website usage data, such as pages visited and general browser/device information, collected automatically when you visit our site.

How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries and provide the services you or a business you're contacting has requested
  • To operate the AI phone answering service on behalf of our clients, including checking appointment availability, booking appointments, and sending appointment-related text messages
  • To improve our services and website
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with the service providers necessary to deliver our services, such as:

  • Twilio, for phone and text messaging infrastructure
  • Square, for appointment scheduling and booking (for clients who use Square)
  • Anthropic, for the AI systems that power our phone answering service
  • n8n, for internal automation and message routing

Each of these providers is only given the information needed to perform its function and is not permitted to use it for its own marketing purposes. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Text messaging

If you receive a text message from a business using MainStreet AI's phone answering service, that message is sent only in connection with an appointment or inquiry you initiated by phone. We do not add phone numbers to marketing text lists, and message data is not used for advertising. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out of future messages, or HELP for assistance.

Data retention

We retain information only as long as necessary to provide our services, meet legal obligations, or resolve disputes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the information below.

Children's privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
